## 📊 Database Scale & Data Coverage

- **1,050,000+ (105万+)** official funded project records.
- **2005–2026** continuous year coverage (including newly gazetted **2026 National Social Science Fund (6,421 projects)**).
- **Three-Tier Funding Sources**: National, Provincial, and Municipal funds.
- **Dual Track Full Integration**:
  - **Natural Sciences (自科)**: National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC, 国家自然科学基金).
  - **Social Sciences (社科)**: National Social Science Fund of China (NSSF, 国家社科基金), Ministry of Education Humanities & Social Sciences (MOE, 教育部人文社科), National Educational Science Planning, China Postdoctoral Science Foundation.

---

## 🌟 Flagship Features

1. **Universal Cross-Source Keyword Search (全库关键词检索)**:
   - Search across all natural science and social science funds simultaneously with zero manual mode switching.
   - Permanently free keyword search by project title, principal investigator (PI), host institution, and subject application codes.
2. **AI Topic Optimization (AI 选题优化)**:
   - Enter your proposed grant topic/title to analyze semantic similarity against the 1.05M+ historical funded project database.
   - Generates structured feasibility reports highlighting innovation angles, differentiation advice, and avoidances.
   - 3 Free Pro-tier reports included for new users.
3. **Official Grant Application Calendar (申报日历)**:
   - Tracks submission deadlines, application guidelines, and review cycles across national, provincial, and departmental grant agencies.
4. **Topic Trend & Funding Analytics (分析报告)**:
   - Visualizes 3-year keyword shifts, emerging funding hotspots (e.g. Artificial Intelligence, New Quality Productive Forces, Digital Economy, Rural Revitalization), and regional/institutional funding distribution charts.
5. **Personal Workspace & Collections (收藏与账号)**:
   - Bookmark candidate project benchmarks, access search history, and manage AI recommendation logs.

## 🇨🇳 中文介绍 · AILatest Grant (科研基金检索与 AI 选题)

### 为什么需要 AILatest Grant？
国家与省部级科研基金申报难度大、周期长。许多科研人员在撰写立项标书时，由于无法全景获知全国近年已有立项情况，经常遭遇“选题撞车”或“偏离资助重点方向”。

**AILatest Grant 打造一站式科研基金情报与 AI 辅助选题平台**：

### 核心能力与数据规模
1. **105万+ 全量立项大数据（2005–2026）**：
   - 覆盖**国家·省·市**三级基金来源；
   - 覆盖国家自然科学基金（自科）、国家社会科学基金（社科，已收录 2026 最新立项公示 6421 项）、教育部人文社科项目、全国教育科学规划课题、中国博士后科学基金及省市重点项目。
2. **全库关键词检索（永久免费）**：
   - 一次性检索自科与社科全部数据源，无需繁琐切换；支持按课题名称、主持人、依托单位及申请代码精确筛选。
3. **AI 选题优化**：
   - 输入拟申报题目，AI 自动调取全库相似立项进行查重与差异化分析，输出结构化立项评估与论证建议；新用户即送 3 次 Pro 级深度报告。
4. **申报日历一览与跟踪**：
   - 汇集国家级、各省科技厅/社科联、高校申报截止时间节点，避免错过申报窗口。
5. **热点趋势与区域高校排行榜**：
   - 掌握人工智能、新质生产力、数字经济等前沿赛道的立项演变与各高校学科获批实力。
